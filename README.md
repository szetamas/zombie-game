This game originally started as a mobile game project (implementation target may change).<br>
The movement system includes wall-jumping and climbing mechanics.<br>
Shooting is currently implemented with a slider button, with light recoil effects. 
Ammo and reloading systems are not yet implemented.<br>
Zombies will actively detect the player within a certain range and move toward them to melee or bite. 
Damage can be direct health loss and/or a chance-based infection system. When player infected, a green fading visual effect appears, 
and the player’s health begins to decreases over time until an antidote item is used from the inventory.<br>
The inventory currently uses a simple UI system with color-coded items. The light blue square for antidote items 
and health is the purple blocks.<br>
The game includes 4 difficulty levels, where enemy behavior, damage values, infection rates, and environmental hazards are different.<br>
Environmental hazards such as spike platforms (red tiles) damage both the player and zombies. 
Additional hazard types will fire, toxic waste, or other environmental dangers.<br>
Zombies currently spawn randomly, but the long-term goal is to design fully structured, playable levels.<br>
Additional mechanics include a dynamic health system where low HP reduces movement speed, 
while overhealed states temporarily increase movement speed before returning to the maximum HP.<br>
This mechanic could be useful in speedrunning.
